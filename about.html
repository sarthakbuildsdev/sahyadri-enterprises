Our Story

About Sahyadri Enterprises

Sahyadri Enterprises is a trusted supplier of premium frozen food products and a dedicated export facilitator. We specialize in delivering high-quality frozen fruits and vegetables while maintaining freshness, hygiene, and international quality standards. Our mission is to provide fresh taste with advanced freezing technology and reliable cold-chain supply.

Beyond frozen foods, we bridge the gap between Indian farmers, producers, and verified global buyers. With a strong network across international markets, we ensure seamless export of agricultural commodities including fresh produce, nuts, pulses, grains, and cereals.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted frozen food supplier and global trade connector, empowering local producers to access international markets with uncompromised quality.

Our Mission

  • Deliver premium frozen foods across India & global markets
  • Maintain highest hygiene & safety standards (FSSAI, HACCP)
  • Ensure 100% customer satisfaction & end-to-end export assistance
  • Connect Indian producers to verified buyers worldwide
